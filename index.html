About

I work across music supervision, sound design, field recording, and audio post-production for film, documentary, commercial, installation, and visual storytelling projects.

From early creative direction to final delivery, I help shape how a project feels. That can mean finding the right musical language, recording real textures on site, building bespoke sonic details, cleaning and editing dialogue, or carrying the full audio package through post-production.

The goal is always the same: create sound that supports the image without overpowering it, adds emotional depth, and gives the story a world the audience can feel inside.

How we work
01

Listen first

Every project begins with deep listening: the brief, the context, the references, and the silence between intentions.

02

Capture the source

When the story needs real texture, we record on site: rooms, air, voices, movement, impacts, and material detail.

03

Build the language

We develop a bespoke sonic vocabulary for the project. Tone, palette, rhythm, music, and space are defined with intention.

04

Deliver with depth

Post-production brings the layers together through editing, cleanup, sound design, mix polish, and final deliverables.
